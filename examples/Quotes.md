# Quotes

Lines that start with `>` are quotes. Put the author on its own line starting with an em dash. Leave a blank line between quotes. Bullets work too.

> Our life is frittered away by detail.
> — Henry David Thoreau, Walden

> Make the quiet thing first, then the loud one.

> Begin at once to live, and count each separate day as a separate life.
> — Seneca
