# Baroque — project notes for Claude Code

Painted, storybook-style iOS home screen widgets fed by an Obsidian vault on GitHub. SwiftUI + WidgetKit, iOS 17+, no third-party dependencies.

## Layout
- `Shared/` compiled into BOTH targets. The widgets are **Rococo** (the user's pick, 2026-11-04), drawn to sit on Hubert Robert's *The Bathing Pool*, the user's wallpaper: `RococoStyle` (ink and type: Didot, Avenir Next, Savoye LET; `RococoFrame`, which lays a widget out at its iPhone 17 design size and scales it to fill the real one; the painted limestone slab, carved lines, the gold sun, the moon, `RococoPicture`, `RococoWords`), `RococoDay` (`RococoDayView(snapshot:quote:size:)`: Sun Arc small, Sun Arc and the days ahead medium, large: one of the user's repainted landscapes in turn, `RococoPhotos.large(at:)`, a new one every three hours, with the date, temperature, a quote and the weather), `RococoToday` (`RococoTodayView`, small, medium with the flower still life, and large: the still life as a band with "Today" and the date, then up to seven tasks that fit (`ViewThatFits`), each with up to two lines, its due note and the brief's source note as a caption via `TaskParser.sourceNote`), `RococoQuote` (`RococoQuoteView(quote:size:date:error:tap:)`: the user's repainted photographs with the quote, which is the next-quote button) and `RococoBattery` (the iPhone's charge). The retired looks left the app on 2026-11-06 for the public release: **Old Master Cover** (`CoverStyle`, `CoverPainting`, `CoverDay`, `CoverQuote`) and the first painted-night views (`Views.swift`) now live in `MockLab/PaintKit/` as real files, their paintings in `MockLab/Archive.xcassets`; the shipped code keeps `BaroqueSize` and its `designSize` in Models and its own words in `RococoWords`. Also Sky (the pieces of the sky the widgets use: `Color(hex:)`, `SkyClock`, `SkyArc`, `MoonPhase`, `MoonLitShape`), Models, Store (App Group `group.com.ryanfernandez.terra` + Keychain token), GitHubClient (Contents API, raw accept header), WeatherClient (Open-Meteo, one request), Parsers (Obsidian Tasks lines + quotes markdown), DataLoader (tasks, quotes, the snapshot cache; a snapshot younger than `DataLoader.freshFor`, 15 minutes, serves every widget, and the app's Save & Refresh passes `maxAge: 0`). Settings decode field by field (`BaroqueSettings.init(from:)`), so add new fields with a default and a line there. `Shared/` holds only what both targets use; the app's painted backdrop is `Baroque/Theme.swift` (palette and materials: brushed panels, raised slabs, carved recesses, copper pebbles, gold frames and sparkles) and `Baroque/Landscape` (the painted sky that follows the time of day and weather, tonight's moon phase, seeded ridges with snowcaps and spruce, mist, and the large Day widget's live sky: sun or moon on today's sunrise-to-sunset arc, clouds, rain, snow; `DayBackground` and `QuoteBackground` are the retired widget grounds; the settings app uses the sky, ridges, sun and moon). MockLab's PaintKit links them from `Baroque/`.
- `Baroque/` the settings app. Tapping the Today widget opens `baroque://brief`, the app's Daily Brief page (`BriefView.swift`: the whole `Daily Brief/Today.md` set out like the note, cached in `Store.brief`, with Open in Obsidian). Also renders live previews of the widgets, holds the user's 24 home screen icons (**Home screen icons**: shared to Photos; `IconFiles` also writes them to Files › On My iPhone › Baroque › Home screen icons) and **Set up your home screen** (`HomeSetup.swift`): how to make each icon as a Shortcuts Open App shortcut with its icon file (web clips were tried and removed on 2026-11-06: they open Safari before the app, and `TargetApplicationBundleIdentifier` needs MDM), saving the wallpaper to Photos, and the author's pages (`HomePlan`, a 4 x 6 grid: page one Day large over eight icons, page two Day, Today and Quote small among twelve). `scripts/mirror_setup.py` drives the phone through iPhone Mirroring (Vision OCR, icon recognition by picture, cliclick); its docstring lists what mirroring does with synthetic input. Docs: `README.md` (public front page, kept short since 2026-11-10 at the user's request: details go in `docs/`, the full credits in `docs/ART.md`), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/SETUP.md` (the step-by-step for a phone), `docs/ART.md`, `docs/WIDGET_IDEAS.md`, `docs/history/` (the original brief and the dev log), `examples/` (vault formats; `examples/Quotes.md` is read by the parser tests). README images: `scripts/readme_images.py` (demo data only).
- `BaroqueWidget/` WidgetKit extension: `BaroqueDay` (small/medium/large), `BaroqueToday` (small/medium/large), `BaroqueQuote` (small/medium/large; the quote is a `NextQuoteIntent` button) `BaroqueBattery` (small; reads `UIDevice` battery each refresh, about every 15 minutes) `BaroqueStillLife` (small and large: only the flower still life, `RococoStillLife*` images, nothing on it) and `BaroquePhotograph` (small: one of the user's repainted photographs, nothing on it, `RococoPhotos.small(at:)`, never the one the large Day shows), all drawing the Rococo views edge to edge (`.contentMarginsDisabled()`).
- `BaroqueUITests/` UI tests that drive SpringBoard to place the widgets on the home screen (used by `scripts/capture_screenshots.sh`).
- `ParserTests/` SwiftPM package that unit tests the real `Shared/` parser files through symlinks: `cd ParserTests && swift test`.
- `MockLab/` (macOS SwiftPM, not part of the Xcode project) holds alternative looks as mocks: one executable target per look (`swift run --scratch-path .build-<look> <Target>` renders the six widgets and a board to `MockLab/out/<slug>/`), a shared harness in `Kit/` (symlinks to the app's Models, Parsers and DemoData; iPhone 17 widget sizes; fixed demo day), and `compose.py` for side-by-side sheets. Mock views are pure SwiftUI so a chosen look ports into `Shared/`; only `Mock.texture` uses AppKit. Painted layout experiments (`Current` = the shipped widgets, rendering `CoverDayView`/`CoverQuoteView` so it can be pixel-compared with `out/old-master-cover/`, `LPanorama`, `LFocus`, `LBento`, `LMedallion`, `LTrail`, `LPlate`) link `PaintKit/` instead, which compiles the app's real Theme, Landscape and Sky, and the retired Views and `Cover*` files and builds `Shared/PaintTextures.xcassets` with actool so `Image("Paint…")` works on macOS; never edit `Shared/` from a mock. Film looks (`NeonRain` and `CrimsonHours` after Wong Kar-wai, `AlpineHotel` and `Expedition` after Wes Anderson) are `Kit/` aesthetics with their own `Textures/<slug>/make.py`. `GrandHotel` is the Grand Budapest look in the shipped painted style: it links `PaintKit/`, and its `make.py` paints new materials with `scripts/make_textures.py`'s stroke painter. `GrandHotelCalm` renders two calmer versions of it, `GCQuiet` and `GCSpare`. It shares GrandHotel's `GHStyle`, `GHScene` and `GHProps` through file symlinks, so keep changes to those files backward compatible: GrandHotel must render identically. `OldMaster` (PaintKit) is the sepia old-master oil-painting look from the user's reference image. Its paintings are grey value studies from `Textures/old-master/make.py`, tinted at run time by `SkyClock`. `OMInscribed`, `OMFolio` and `OMCover` are more inventive compositions of it. They symlink `../OldMaster/OMStyle.swift`, so keep that file stable, and import Old Master's `make.py` for their paintings. `OMCover` is the user's pick and now ships as `Shared/Cover*.swift`; the mock stays as the reference. Its mountain is the Matterhorn, its skyline traced from a photograph taken at the Riffelsee and stored in `Textures/old-master-cover/matterhorn.json`. Don't swap in invented crests. `compose.py` puts all of these with `Current` in `out/films/`. The weather-first mocks (`WSunArc`, `WAlmanac`, `WGiltFrame`, `WStorybook`, `WCelestial`, `WPoster`, `WNumeral`) come from the 2026-11-04 brief. Each has a Weather widget (temperature, condition, sunrise and sunset, month; no calendar grid, no tasks) and a separate To-do widget. They conform to `WeatherMock` in `Kit/WeatherMockKit.swift` (also linked into PaintKit), and `WeatherRunner` writes `weather-<size>[-night].png`, `todo-<size>.png` and a board. `SA_HOME=1` (`Sources/WSunArc/SAHome.swift`, paintings in `Textures/weather-sun-arc/home/`) renders the planned home screen. Page one has Sun Arc large with a quote as its only widget. Page two is a checkerboard of widgets and apps: to-do, a picture, and a forecast/moon stack. Custom 3D icons sit on a Baroque cloud wallpaper. `SA_TRAV=1` (`TVHome.swift`, materials in `Textures/travertine/`) renders the travertine look from the user's second pin: stone tiles, raised reliefs and sunlit paintings, as a showcase plus both pages, in `out/travertine/`. Its app icons are 3D models rendered in Blender (`Textures/travertine/icons/build.py`, run headless with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P`). Edit the script and rerun it rather than editing the PNGs. The Pinterest research behind them was kept in the session scratchpad (`pinterest-research/`). The pages use the user's own apps and layout (`TVApps`: dock, page one large widget over eight apps, page two medium/eight apps/medium, page three medium over four); `icons/export.py [travertine|rococo]` writes them for the Shortcuts app to `screenshots/<set>/icons-for-shortcuts/`. `SA_ROCOCO=1` (`RCHome.swift`, `TVStyle.rococo`) draws the same set over Hubert Robert's The Bathing Pool, to `out/rococo/`: stones oil-painted in its colours by `Textures/rococo/make.py`, Didot, icons from `ICON_SET=rococo` build.py, the user's outdoor photographs (read from `~/Pictures`, processed locally, never uploaded) repainted by `Textures/rococo/photos.py` for the pictures, the flower still life in the Today panel, and the pages again on Fragonard's The Swing and the still life as alternative wallpapers. `RSmalls` (links PaintKit plus `Shared/RococoStyle.swift`) draws ten small widget ideas in the shipped rococo look to `out/smalls/` (listed in `WIDGET_IDEAS.md`).
- `project.yml` is the source of truth for `Baroque.xcodeproj` (xcodegen). If you add a Swift file, run `xcodegen generate` and reopen. The signing team comes from `Config/Local.xcconfig` (git-ignored; `Config/Baroque.xcconfig` includes it; copy `Local.example.xcconfig`), never from project.yml. Keep both targets, the App Group entitlement on both, and the shared sources in both.
- `Shared/PaintTextures.xcassets` (widget paint textures and relief overlays) and `Baroque/Assets.xcassets` App* images come from `scripts/make_textures.py` (numpy, scipy, Pillow). Edit the script and rerun it rather than editing the images. Keep widget textures near 1 px per point: WidgetKit refuses to render widgets whose images are too large. The Cover paintings are painted by `MockLab/Textures/old-master-cover/make.py` and copied into `MockLab/Archive.xcassets` as `Cover*` imagesets by `scripts/export_cover_textures.py` (`--paint` repaints first; deterministic, offline). The rococo widgets' images (`Rococo*` in `PaintTextures.xcassets`, 2 px per point, JPEG), the app icon (Sun Arc modelled in Blender) and the user's 24 home screen icons (`Baroque/Assets.xcassets/HomeIcons`, which the app's "Home screen icons" section shares to Photos for Shortcuts) come from `scripts/export_rococo_textures.py`, which reads MockLab's rococo paintings, repainted photos and Blender renders. Edit the sources and rerun it rather than editing the images.
- DEBUG launch arguments: `-resetData` (fresh-install state), `-seedDemoData` (demo tasks and quotes in the cache) and `-renderWidgets` (exact-size PNGs of every widget state at three phone sizes, plus `fit.txt` noting each size's scale), handled in `Baroque/DebugLaunch.swift` and `Baroque/WidgetRenders.swift`.

## Data conventions (from the author's vault repository)
- Tasks: `- [ ] #todo Text ⏫ 📅 2026-06-05` (Obsidian Tasks plugin). Source since 2026-11-06: `Daily Brief/Today.md`, which the user's morning brief rewrites and pushes each day (its tasks sit under "Needs attention", undated; the schedule and notes below are plain bullets the parser skips). Settings still holding the old default are moved to it once (`Store.settings`). Before that: `Tasks/Master Task List.md` plus today's daily note at `daily note/MM.dd.yyyy - EEEE.md` (e.g. `daily note/12.30.2025 - Tuesday.md`). Priority emoji: 🔺 ⏫ 🔼 🔽 ⏬. Done tasks `- [x]` are ignored.
- Quotes: `main/Quotes/Quotes.md` (the user's file, created 2026-11-05; the app's default path), blockquotes with an author line starting with a dash, blank line between quotes; bullets `- "text" — Author` and plain lines that open with a quotation mark (`“text” - Author`, the user's own style) also parse. Headings and other prose are ignored, so the file can be grouped under headings. `Quotes.md` at the repo root is a starter (the parser tests read it).
- Weather: Open-Meteo, no key (current conditions, today's high/low, sunrise and sunset). Location is stored once from the app (CoreLocation) or typed manually.

## Rules
- The app was called Terra until 2026-11-05, when the user asked to "rename everything so its baroque as the name". The project, targets, folders, types, widget kinds (`BaroqueDay`, `BaroqueToday`, `BaroqueQuote`, `BaroqueBattery`), storage keys and docs say Baroque. The bundle IDs (`com.ryanfernandez.terra`, `.widgets`, `.uitests`), the App Group `group.com.ryanfernandez.terra` and the Keychain service keep `terra`, because the original brief says not to change bundle identifiers unless a build forces it. This folder is `~/claudeProjects/Baroque` (it was `~/claudeProjects/Terra` until 2026-11-10). The repo is `RyeBreadFernandez/baroque`, public since 2026-11-06.
- Never commit a GitHub token anywhere. It is entered in the app and stored in the Keychain.
- Photographs take turns (`RococoPhotos` in RococoStyle: every three hours on the local clock). The large Day widget's list (`underWords`) holds only landscapes with no one where the quote sits; check any new one with MockLab `HEROES=1 swift run --scratch-path .build-smalls RSmalls` (every photo by day and night). Photos of people go only on the Photograph widget. New photos: `photos.py`, then `PHOTOS` in `scripts/export_rococo_textures.py`.
- The widgets' surface is `RococoTheme.current` in RococoStyle: **Quiet** since 2026-11-08, after the user's art redirect ("the effect needs to be a lot more subtle" so the words read; "a little bit more that makes them stand out"). Quiet is a light limestone (#DCD1BB) with the painted brushwork at 28 percent, darker inks (every text colour 4.5:1 or better on the stone), and photographs darkened 1.3 times more behind their words. The other options (Current, Carved with a groove round the inside, Ivory with a crisp outline) stay in `RococoTheme.options`; compare with MockLab `THEMES=1 swift run --scratch-path .build-smalls RSmalls` and `scripts/compare_themes.py`. Keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or better on the stone.
- Keep the rococo look: limestone with quiet oil brushwork, carved lines and gold reliefs; the user's own outdoor photographs repainted in the colours of The Bathing Pool and The Swing (`MockLab/Textures/rococo/photos.py`, run locally: the photos stay on this Mac); Didot, Avenir Next capitals, Savoye LET only for short lines. No weekday name anywhere, no gold frames. Words on a picture sit on its darkened side; check them at night (pictures are glazed darker) and on long quotes (they shrink, then truncate).
- If the Old Master Cover look comes back, keep it: sepia oils in umber, taupe and cream with one peach accent (`CoverInk`), paintings as value studies glazed at run time for the time of day and weather (never flat fills or plain gradients), varnish and craquelure, the giant gilt date numeral (or quote mark) standing beside the Matterhorn's summit with its foot behind the mountain, Baskerville and Avenir Next type. Text sits on the painting's dark ground; anything set on the sky uses `.coverOnSky()`/`.coverOnPaint()`. Check contrast on the lightest paint. No weekday name anywhere.
- Keep the Matterhorn in the shipped Cover paintings. (The user picked the Sun Arc weather mock on 2026-11-04 and said the scenery "doesnt just have to be matterhorn". Its other landscapes live in `MockLab/Textures/weather-sun-arc/scenes/<scene>/`, each rendered with `SA_SCENE=<scene>`, and follow the same rule: a real place in its classic view, its silhouette traced from a credited, freely licensed photograph, at true proportions. They have no gold frames, which the user dislikes.) In Cover, every painting's mountain is its classic Riffelsee silhouette from `MockLab/Textures/old-master-cover/matterhorn.json` (traced from Wikimedia Commons `File:Matterhorn_Riffelsee_2005-06-11.jpg`; `make.py --trace <skyline.json>` rebuilds it), at its true proportions: scale it uniformly, never stretch or exaggerate it, and never invent a crest. Frame it (`FRAMES` in `make.py`) so the hooked summit, the steep left face and the shoulder on the right stay clear of the glyph and the text; the glyph's foot may sit behind the flank or shoulder. Light it as in the photograph: the left face in shadow, the east and north faces lit with snow, a crisp crest. Layouts are fixed at the iPhone 17 sizes and each painting is composed round its text and glyph (`HEROES` in `make.py` must match `OCCanvas.hero` in the mock and `CoverCanvas.hero` in `Shared/`). If you move text or the glyph, repaint with `make.py`, run `scripts/export_cover_textures.py`, and check the `-renderWidgets` output (including the `sky-*` weather states) and MockLab `Current` against `out/old-master-cover/`.
- Widgets must render with cached data when offline and show a readable error line when a fetch fails.
- Build for the simulator first (no signing needed). Device install: the team in `Config/Local.xcconfig`, the App Group ticked once in Signing & Capabilities for both targets, then `scripts/install_on_iphone.sh` (Release by default: Debug SwiftUI is noticeably laggy on the phone; `CONFIGURATION=Debug` for the debug launch arguments the mirroring script uses).
- Keep the app's main screen light to scroll: the previews are one Equatable view (`WidgetPreviews`) so settings edits don't redraw them, and the previews, painted background, row slabs and plan pages are each flattened with `.drawingGroup()`. Lists of icons use `IconThumbnail` (cached 144 px thumbnails), never the 512 px icons.
- The repo is meant to be public: no tokens, no real names, tasks or quotes from the vault in tests, demo data or images (the README's pictures use demo data), no personal paths. Demo quotes are public domain and correctly attributed.
