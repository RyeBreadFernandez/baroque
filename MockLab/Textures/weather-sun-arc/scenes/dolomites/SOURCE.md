# Dolomites: source

**Subject:** the Tre Cime di Lavaredo (Drei Zinnen). This is the classic view of the three north faces from near the Locatelli hut (Dreizinnenhütte), looking south-west:
- Cima Piccola on the left;
- Cima Grande (2,999 m) in the middle;
- Cima Ovest on the right.

Left is east: the morning sun lights the towers' eastern faces while the north faces stand in shadow. The sun sets on the right.

**Reference photograph:** [File:Tremi cime di Lavaredo del rifugio Locatelli.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tremi_cime_di_Lavaredo_del_rifugio_Locatelli.jpg) on Wikimedia Commons
- Author: Irene Scarpante
- Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Taken July 2016.

**What was taken from it:**
- **The skyline.** For every column of a 3840 px wide copy (columns 0 to 3300), the trace records the first row that is not blue sky.
- **Lines drawn by eye on the same photograph:**
  - each tower's columns;
  - its main vertical arête, which splits its sunlit face from its shadowed one;
  - the row where the sun leaves the lower faces;
  - the scree fans at the towers' feet.

All of it is stored in `dolomites.json`, normalised. The units are Cima Grande's north face from the summit to the scree at its foot (772 px). No pixels of the photograph are kept.

**Painted, not traced:**
- each tower in two clean planes split along its arête, sunlit and shadowed;
- the fluting and chimneys, as long vertical streaks;
- a few crisp ledges;
- a lit rim along the sunlit skyline;
- the scree fans catching the light;
- the stony plateau in front, its rim lit in the middle and the dark slope below it.

**Framing:**
- Cima Grande's summit sits at (0.438, 0.292), at 0.135 pt per photo px, with the skyline scaled uniformly.
- The group sits a little left of the crown so the arc's right foot clears the west tower's shoulder.
- The scree at the feet is at about 0.58.

**Arc:** the Matterhorn's, (0.5, 0.575, 0.42, 0.45). There is no `scene.json`.

**Shared painter:** `../fuji/world.py`.
