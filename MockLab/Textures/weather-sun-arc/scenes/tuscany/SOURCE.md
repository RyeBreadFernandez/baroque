# Tuscany: source

**Subject:** the Val d'Orcia. The scene shows the cypress knoll of San Quirico d'Orcia (the "Cipressini"), a ring of Italian cypresses on a low rise among ploughed fields, under a wide sky. There is no peak: the hills and the cypresses are the subject. The afternoon sun is on the right (west), catching the trees' west edges, and it sets on the right.

**What was taken from it:**
- **The skyline.** For every column of a 3840 px wide copy, the trace records the first row that is not blue sky: the far hills and the clump's outline.
- **The cypress tops.** These are the skyline's local highs (`tops`).
- **Two things drawn by eye:** the foot of the knoll under the trees (`knoll`) and the lone cypress on the far hill (`lone`).

All of it is stored in `tuscany.json`, normalised. The units are the tallest cypress's height above the knoll (487 px). No pixels of the photograph are kept.

**The cypresses:** each is a crisp, narrow flame standing at its traced top, with its traced height down to the knoll and a slightly irregular outline. Sky shows between some of them. Each has a thin warm rim down its west edge and a few dark vertical strokes inside.

**Painted, not traced:**
- the far hill running on behind the clump;
- the knoll's soft lit crest, with the trees' shadows beyond it to the left;
- on the right, a swell that rises behind the knoll to carry the arc's right foot. In the photograph the ground falls away there, about 20 pt lower at the foot;
- one long rolling hill line in front, with a white road winding over it toward the knoll.

Every field has furrows that follow the curve of the land, and all of it darkens to the ground the type sits on.

**Framing:**
- The tallest top sits at (0.596, 0.411), at 0.15 pt per photo px, with the skyline scaled uniformly.
- The clump is centred under the crown, and the far hill on the left meets the arc's left foot.

**Arc:** the Matterhorn's, (0.5, 0.575, 0.42, 0.45). There is no `scene.json`.
